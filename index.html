Hermes Agent

A personal, single-user automation client for Google Workspace.

What this is

Hermes Agent is a command-line AI assistant running locally on its owner's own computer. This site exists to document the application for the Google OAuth consent screen and to publish its privacy policy. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public as a service.

What it does with Google data

With the owner's explicit permission, the application accesses the owner's own Google account to:

  • Read, search, send, and label Gmail messages
  • Read and create Google Calendar events
  • Access files in the owner's Google Drive
  • Read and write Google Sheets and Google Docs
  • Read the owner's Google Contacts

All data stays on the owner's device. Nothing is transmitted to any third party, and there is no analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.

Who can use it

One person: the account owner. There is no sign-up, no multi-tenant service, and no other users' data is ever touched.
